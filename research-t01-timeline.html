Calibrating AI Auditors Before Trusting Their Judgements

If LLMs are used to evaluate other AI systems, the evaluator itself first becomes a measurement-design problem.

Period · Feb 2026 – Aug 2026 Type · AI Evaluation · LLM-as-Judge · Self-Determination Theory Evaluation set · 30 human participants · 30 synthetic users
Research question

How can synthetic users and AI auditors be calibrated against human participants before we rely on them for pre-deployment evaluation?

01 · RISK

Before giving AI to young people

A psychologically sensitive conversational system creates an uncomfortable evaluation problem: discovering a failure through repeated exposure can itself place vulnerable participants at risk.

Technical testing is necessary, but it cannot tell us whether an interaction makes someone feel controlled, incapable, misunderstood, dismissed, or pushed toward action before they are ready.

Synthetic userEncounters experimental agent variants
AI auditorEstimates need support / frustration
Human benchmarkCalibrates whether synthetic judgement is trustworthy

The aim was not to replace human evaluation. Thirty human participants remained the benchmark, with 30 synthetic AI users used to test calibration.

02 · PERSONA

Encode causes, not conclusions

A synthetic persona can accidentally contain the answer to the outcome we later ask it to evaluate. If a profile explicitly says a person dislikes being told what to do, asking whether an agent supported their autonomy risks prompting the result rather than simulating it.

AvoidPersona statements that directly encode the psychological judgement being measured.
PreferUpstream characteristics — everyday context, symptoms, emotion regulation, dispositions, self-efficacy, stress.

A synthetic user should contain conditions that may shape an outcome. It should not contain the outcome we are trying to predict.

03 · CALIBRATION

The evaluator is part of the measurement design

The observed judgement changed when we changed how the evaluation was framed.

Measurement levelBehaviour → taskTask-level judgement reduced aggregate error relative to broader behaviour-level constructs.
PerspectiveThird person → first personAsking the synthetic participant to report its own experience brought estimates closer to the human benchmark.
ContextThin → situatedContext improved first-person evaluation across autonomy, competence, and relatedness.
EvaluatorGenerator → stronger reasonerA stronger evaluation model moved closer to the human benchmark.

An AI auditor is not a neutral ruler. Perspective, abstraction, context, and model capability all affect what it measures.

04 · INSTRUMENT

Synthetic user as an experimental instrument

It is less useful to think of the synthetic user as a fixed artificial person and more useful to think of the whole configuration as a measurement instrument.

Synthetic response = Person × Context × Measurement × Model

Who the persona is matters, but so does the situation, the question, the abstraction level, the perspective from which it’s answered, and the model that performs the reasoning.

Changing any one part can change the synthetic “participant” we think we are observing.

05 · BENCHMARK

How close could the final configuration get?

The final synthetic configuration was compared with mean METUX ratings from the 30 human participants.

06 · BOUNDARY

A mismatch can define the boundary of trust

Autonomy and competence leave substantial evidence in the observable interaction. Relatedness can depend more strongly on whether somebody felt cared for, connected, or personally understood.

Use synthetic evaluation forRepeated pre-deployment testing, surfacing failure modes, comparing configurations.
Keep humans essential forConstructs and experiences synthetic evaluation cannot reproduce with acceptable calibration.

The goal is not “Can AI replace participants?” It is “What psychological failures can we surface before a vulnerable participant encounters the system?”

Design takeaway

Synthetic evaluation should not be trusted because it looks human-like. It should be trusted only to the extent that it has been calibrated against humans.
